Cryptoninja Emaki — Support

Last updated: 2026-07-29

About this app

This is a single-player tower-defense game that runs completely offline. It makes no network requests at all. No account or sign-in is required, and it shows no advertising.

Q. Do I need an internet connection?

No. Once installed, the app never communicates over the network.

Q. Do I need to create an account?

No account is required and there is no sign-in feature.

Q. Where is my progress stored?

Only on your device. Nothing is ever sent to an external server. See the Privacy Policy page for details.

Q. How do I erase my progress?

Choose はじめから (Start Over) under 音と設定 (Sound & Settings) inside the app to erase all stored progress and settings. Deleting the app has the same effect. Erased progress cannot be restored.

Q. Are there any in-app purchases?

The current version is free, with no in-app purchases.

Q. I found a bug, or I have feedback

Please contact us at the address below. Including which chapter and battle you were in when the issue occurred helps a great deal.
